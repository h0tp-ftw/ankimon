## 🌟 Ankimon v2.2-E 🌟

A huge thank you to [@Nut2026](https://github.com/Nut2026) (Nuz), [@h0tp-ftw](https://github.com/h0tp-ftw) (h0tp), [@hakimh2](https://github.com/hakimh2) (Brrrrrrr), [@scotej](https://github.com/scotej) (Scoot) for their contributions to this update! <3

This release includes 26 merged pull requests with 5 enhancements and 7 bug fixes!

This update focuses on making Ankimon safer, smoother, and more reliable during everyday play. Your Pokémon data is now better protected during syncing, with improved handling for cloud-storage conflicts and tools to help detect and repair database problems. Level-ups, Battle Power, mobile reviews, Pokémon stats, and PC Box updates should behave more consistently, including fixes for runaway levels and outdated CP values. The update also adds more control over which battle-screen elements are shown, improves Pokémon sprite updates, and resolves several crashes and display issues. Overall, Ankimon should feel more stable when reviewing cards, switching devices, updating resources, and managing your collection.
