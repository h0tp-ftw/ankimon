## 🌟 Ankimon v2.31-E 🌟

A huge thank you to [@Copilot](https://github.com/Copilot), [@Nut2026](https://github.com/Nut2026) (Nuz), [@h0tp-ftw](https://github.com/h0tp-ftw) (h0tp), [@scotej](https://github.com/scotej) (Scoot), [@slager2](https://github.com/slager2) (slager) for their contributions to this update! <3

This release includes 11 merged pull requests with 2 enhancements and 8 bug fixes!

This update introduces on-demand capture and defeat options alongside a new setting to display Pokémon sprites across all screens. We've also resolved in-app updater conflicts with AnkiWeb, fixed battle window cropping, and addressed bugs with Gen 9 evolutions, nature resets, and Discord Rich Presence stability.
