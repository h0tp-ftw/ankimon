### What's new
Ankimon v2 is finally here! Thanks to our contributors around the world, we have crushed a ton of bugs and added amazing new features to make your study sessions even more fun. Battles are now smoother and fairer than ever, with smarter damage calculations and status effects working exactly as they should. You can now customize your Pokémon with held items and TMs from our expanded item shop, and organize your collection perfectly using the new PC Box features. We've also added brand new achievements to earn, and made the Pokédex load lightning fast so you can view your catches instantly. Best of all, Ankimon is now more reliable than ever, with the option to opt-in and seamlessly sync your progress across devices! Catch 'em all!

— h0tp 💖
