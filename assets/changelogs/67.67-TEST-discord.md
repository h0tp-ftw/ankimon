## 🌟 Ankimon v67.67-TEST 🌟

A huge thank you to AIbrahimv2, MrElektronz, aaaaankier, Unlucky, Zuukiny, abr-projects, gjgress, h0tp, hakimh2, maciej-baruch, mathieulabs, and scotej for their contributions to this update! <3

### What's new
There are massive improvements in this release! Data has been successfully migrated to SQL and we finally have an awesome **In-App Update System** alongside CP tracking similar to Pokemon GO.

We've completely rewritten generation-aware learnsets, refactored the underlying engine, and introduced a **Lucky Egg** functionality!

And, of course, a huge shoutout to my favorite bugfix: finally preventing lookup crashes for hyphenated Pokemon names! That bug was super annoying but is history now!

— h0tp 💖
