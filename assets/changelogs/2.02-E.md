## 🌟 Ankimon v2.02-E 🌟

A huge thank you to [@h0tp-ftw](https://github.com/h0tp-ftw) (h0tp), [@hakimh2](https://github.com/hakimh2) (Brrrrrrr), [@scotej](https://github.com/scotej) (Scoot) for their contributions to this update! <3

### What's new
This release focuses on making evolution smoother and fixing critical issues! First off, the infinite loop during Pokemon generation is gone, giving you better stability. Also, time-of-day and friendship evolution mechanics have been greatly enhanced—you can now track your Pokemon's BFF status right in the UI! We've also implemented cached lookups to cut down on disk I/O, which means faster performance across the board. And lastly, enjoy a balanced economy with a cap of 400¥ for daily trainer cash earnings! 💰
